## Brand & Style
The design system transforms study and exam preparation into an inspiring, deep-space intellectual journey. Designed specifically for high school students tackling the rigorous Romanian Baccalaureate, the product strips away the anxiety, sterile monotony, and intimidation of traditional textbooks. In their place, it creates an immersive command deck—an interface where AI analysis feels like peering into an illuminated constellation of thought.

The design movement combines **Futuristic Glassmorphism** with **Deep Space Cyber-Minimalism**. Dense cosmic backdrops allow luminous accents, nebula purples, and stellar cyans to guide optical hierarchy without eye strain during nocturnal study marathons. The atmosphere balances the gravitas of deep space with the sharp precision of state-of-the-art telemetry tools.

## Colors
The palette evokes an atmospheric cosmic void enriched with vibrant spectral radiation:

- **Cosmic Canvas & Deep Tiers**: The base background sits at `#0A071B`, layered with container depths of `#0E0927` and translucent elevated surfaces (`#161036` at varying opacities).
- **Nebula Primaries**: `#7C3AED` (Deep Violet) and `#A855F7` (Luminous Amethyst) provide dominant visual authority, energy fields, and active states.
- **Stellar Cyans & Blues**: `#06B6D4` (Supernova Cyan) and `#3B82F6` (Orbital Azure) highlight analytical tags, progress markers, AI scoring criteria, and targeted feedback indicators.
- **Electric Accent**: `#8B5CF6` combined with radial gradients of `#EC4899` (used sparingly as stardust highlights) signals critical evaluation highlights.
- **Text & Foreground Hierarchy**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for primary headings, `#E2E8F0` for body typography, and `#94A3B8` for secondary captions, metadata, and placeholder text.

## Typography
Typography creates a strong architectural hierarchy between technical data and long-form analysis. 

- **Space Grotesk** commands the system's display, headlines, button actions, tab navigations, and telemetry indicators. Its geometric, slightly mechanical proportions reinforce the futuristic sci-fi terminal vibe.
- **Inter** handles essay composition, prompt assistance, barem explanations, and continuous prose. Its neutral, highly legible anatomy ensures sustained reading comfort when analyzing complex Romanian literary works and argumentative texts.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by a centered container with a maximum width of 1440px.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (up to 640px, 4 columns), Tablet (641px–1024px, 8 columns), and Desktop (1025px+, 12 columns).
- **Desktop Workflow**: Employs a dual-viewport or split-pane paradigm where source text / essay inputs occupy the left 6 or 7 columns, while rubric evaluation, grading bars, and AI telemetry occupy the right 5 or 6 columns.
- **Rhythm & Structure**: Spacing utilizes strict 8pt base unit increments (0.5rem, 1rem, 1.5rem, 2.5rem) to ensure structural balance between glassmorphic panels and dense interactive controls.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons conventional heavy drop-shadows in favor of translucent luminous glassmorphism and subtle boundary backlights:

- **Base Level (Canvas)**: Deep void gradient from `#0A071B` through `#0E0927`, enriched with subtle, large-radius atmospheric radial blurs (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08)` and `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.05)`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Shells)**: Translucent glass containers (`background: rgba(14, 9, 39, 0.65)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), encased by delicate borders (`1px solid rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.18)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Inputs, Sliders & Cards)**: Inset darker layers (`rgba(10, 7, 27, 0.75)`) or elevated tiles framed with refined borders (`1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.28)`).
- **Glow & Starlight Elevation**: Interactive primary focal points generate directional colored ambient glows:
  - Primary button / Active states: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.5), 0 0 8px 0 rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`.
  - Focused inputs: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.8), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`.

## Shapes
The shape language uses balanced rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`). Panels, interactive inputs, and modal envelopes share an 8px (`0.5rem`) base curve, extending to 16px (`1rem`) on outer modular cards and feature containers. 

Pill forms are reserved strictly for status chips, rubric score badges, and segmented tab switchers to provide rhythmic contrast against the architectural rectangular input viewports.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action ("Verifică", "Generează text")**: Gradient surface spanning from `#7C3AED` to `#6366F1` with an interior top specular highlight (`border-t: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`). Accompanied by a subtle cyan-to-violet drop glow. Hover scales subtly (`scale: 1.01`) and intensifies the outer aura (`0 0 28px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.6)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Frosted background (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.12)`), text in `#E2E8F0`, bounded by `1px solid rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.25)`. Hover fills with `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)` and brightens the border.

### Input Fields & Textareas
- Built for deep-focus essay writing. Background sits at `rgba(10, 7, 27, 0.85)` with a frosted rim `1px solid rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.2)`. 
- Placeholder text in `#64748B`. 
- On focus, transition border to `#06B6D4` with a radiant cyan bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`). Font stays clean, monospaced-adjusted Inter with generous line-height (`1.65`).

### Navigation Tabs
- Underline indicator is replaced by a floating glowing segment pill or high-tech glass bar. Active tab displays `#FFFFFF` text, a gradient under-glow in Supernova Cyan (`#06B6D4`), and a translucent backing (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.2)`). Inactive tabs sit comfortably at `#94A3B8`.

### Sliders & Range Controls
- Sliders (temperature, character count) use a sleek 4px track styled in dark nebula purple (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)`), with an active fill gradient of `#06B6D4` to `#A855F7`.
- Thumbs are luminous orbs (`16px`) with a solid white center, wrapped in an electric cyan glow ring.

### Analysis Cards & Barem Results
- Modular breakdown cards for paragraph scoring and criterion validation. Outfitted with neon edge-accents (cyan for passing/strong points, amber for warnings, violet for style feedback).
- Embedded metric tags (e.g., "Structură", "Argumentare", "Citate") utilize pill shapes with saturated micro-indicators.
